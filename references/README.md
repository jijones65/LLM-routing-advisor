# Skills reference examples

These files were supplied by the project owner and reviewed on 19 August 2026 as illustrative inputs for the application's Skills taxonomy:

- `ai_capabilities_matrix.xlsx` — examples of technical, operational, scientific and domain AI capabilities;
- `bpa_categories.xlsx` — examples of activities that may be supported by business-process automation; and
- `illustrative_ai_activities_tasks_skills.pdf` — an illustrative SFIA-oriented view of AI process, people, data, technology and governance work.

They are examples, not product instructions, a complete list, an authoritative standard or proof of model performance.

The product uses them to find missing **transferable application behaviours**, such as anomaly detection, optimisation, simulation, workflow approvals, system integration, process improvement, data quality, service monitoring, sensor use and human escalation. Specialised industry workflows remain examples unless the model catalogue contains evidence that can distinguish model variants for that work. People, organisational change, accountability and governance remain implementation responsibilities and context requirements rather than model Skills.
